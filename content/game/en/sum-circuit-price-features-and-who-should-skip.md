## What Sum Circuit is

Sum Circuit is a puzzle game in the Games and Puzzle genres, published by Sae Won Yang and available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and iPod touch. The core loop is arithmetic and gravity: you select 2–4 neighbouring tiles whose values add up to the target sum, connect them, and those tiles clear. Because numbers above fall into the gaps, clearing a tile now can change what is available for the next target. You work through targets from left to right to light the circuit.

The listing describes three ways to play. The 30-stage Journey opens with a short interactive tutorial and then moves through multiple targets and different connection shapes. The 365-puzzle Expedition unlocks one circuit at a time, asks you to complete each sector, and saves your best star result for every puzzle. Daily and Bonus circuits give you a date-based puzzle, and completing the Daily Circuit unlocks its bonus for free.

Controls are deliberately plain: tap tiles and press Connect, or drag across them and release. Undo and restart are available when a route dead-ends. Modes, stage selection and game tools live in Settings rather than on the play screen, so the game screen stays focused. You can also repeat the tutorial without losing your current puzzle.
