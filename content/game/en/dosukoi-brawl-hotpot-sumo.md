Dosukoi Brawl: Hotpot Sumo is a 3D physics party game where sushi, pudding, a Moai and a rubber duck shove each other off a sumo ring. It is listed as free on the App Store for iOS, and it is not out yet: the pre-order release date is 2026-10-31. The pitch is simple — knock rivals into the hotpot soup, and whoever scores the most knockouts wins.

A match runs on three controls: move with your left thumb, hold the tackle button to charge and release to dash, and jump to dodge. Up to four players join through a 4-digit room code, with CPUs filling empty spots and no login or sign-up needed. The listing does not show how long a single match or session lasts.

## What Dosukoi Brawl: Hotpot Sumo Is and What You Actually Do

The core action is a push-out sumo round: you charge a tackle, release it into a rival, and try to send them off the ring and into the soup. Knockback grows the more hits you take and the less time is left, so a player who is behind can still fly back into the match until the last second. Emotes are there to liven up a room, and the listing does not say which emotes exist or how they are unlocked.

Four stages change the footing rather than the rules. Dosukoi Ring is classic push-out sumo, Crumbling Island drops its floor away, Slippery Ice adds ice you cannot stop on plus a giant squeaky hammer, and Zero-G Bombs runs on floaty low gravity with a rain of bombs. What the game does not state is how many rounds a full match takes or whether stage order is fixed.
