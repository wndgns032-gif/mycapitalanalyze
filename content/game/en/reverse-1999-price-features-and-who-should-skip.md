## What Reverse: 1999 Is
Reverse: 1999 is a role-playing game in the strategic, turn-based mould, set in an alternate 20th century. You play the Timekeeper, an observer of eras who can travel through time after each "Storm," working with Sonetto, an Arcanist and ally from the Foundation. Your mission is to reach the eras where the "Storm" is most active, find other Arcanists who can sense it coming, and save them from being erased from the timeline. The store category is role-playing, and the listing frames the whole thing as a cinematic adventure spanning from the Roaring '20s to the turn of the millennium. It is a live game with an ongoing story, not a self-contained single-player campaign.

## Key Features
- Travel through 20th century eras as the Timekeeper, from the 1920s to the year 1999, chasing the truth about the "Storm."
- Fight turn-based battles by building a team, casting spells and using arcane skills against enemies.
