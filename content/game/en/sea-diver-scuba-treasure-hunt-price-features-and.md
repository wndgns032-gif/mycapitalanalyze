Sea Diver: Scuba Treasure Hunt is an upcoming casual adventure game from developer Vitalii Chardymov, listed on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. The premise is simple: one tank of air, a whole ocean of treasure, and a diver who has to get back to the boat before the gauge hits empty. The official page confirms it is free to download and that it contains ads plus optional in-app purchases.

The game is built around repeat dives rather than a single story. Each trip earns coins, each coin buys better gear, and better gear takes the diver deeper. It is currently listed for pre-order with a release date of 23 October 2026.

## What Sea Diver: Scuba Treasure Hunt Is

Sea Diver: Scuba Treasure Hunt is a casual scuba diving game where you tap DIVE, steer a diver through reefs, shipwrecks and sunken ruins, and fill a loot bag with pearls and pirate gold. The store categories are Games, Casual and Adventure, and the developer is Vitalii Chardymov. Each dive ends when the air gauge runs out or when you return to the boat, and running out costs you the diver along with everything in the bag.

The loop is risk against reward on every single dive: crack one more chest or play it safe and surface. Coins from the haul pay for a bigger air tank, faster fins, a wetsuit, a harpoon gun, nets and a flashlight. The listing does not show a story campaign or a named protagonist.

## Key Features

- Explore hand-crafted underwater worlds of 50 to 150 screens per location, covering reefs, kelp forests, caves and wrecks.
- Collect white, pink, black and rare golden pearls, plus coral, starfish, amphorae, coins, gems, idols and crowns.
- Manage a draining air gauge, with currents, growing darkness and a flashlight for deeper water.
- Face reef sharks, great whites, Greenland sharks, barracudas, jellyfish, moray eels, pufferfish, leopard seals and anglerfish.
- Fight back with a harpoon gun, nets and shark repellent, or ride friendly turtles and dolphins.
- Upgrade gear and the boat, trading a dinghy for a trawler, speedboat, yacht and finally a golden yacht.
