Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000 is an upcoming free-to-play strategy game from Stillfront Supremacy GmbH. It is set in the Warhammer 40,000 universe and is built around long-term multiplayer conflict on the world of Vigilus. The official page says it is not released yet and is open for pre-registration.

This is not a solo story campaign. Matches are described as running over days or weeks with up to 64 players. The page does not show a release date, a price for anything beyond the base download, or a supported device list.

## What Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000 Is

Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000 is a multiplayer strategy game from the creators of Supremacy 1914. It moves that long-term formula into the grim darkness of the far future, on the war-torn world of Vigilus. The official page describes it as a long-term, multiplayer strategy experience where tactics, diplomacy and domination decide the fate of the world.

At launch you lead one of four factions: Space Marines, Chaos Space Marines, Orks or Astra Militarum. Battles take place on 30 or 64-player maps that evolve over days or weeks. The page also links to a website, X, Reddit, Facebook, YouTube and Discord for the game.

## Release Date and Pre-Order

The official page does not show a release date, so there is no confirmed day to plan around. What it does show is a pre-registration option: you can register now to be notified when the battle begins. The page calls the game Coming Soon.

There is no mention of a pre-order price, a pre-order edition or any pre-registration reward. Because the listing is on Google Play, any date shown there would be an update date rather than a release date, and this listing shows none. Treat the launch window as unknown until the developer states one.

## Key Features

- Pick one of four factions at launch: Space Marines, Chaos Space Marines, Orks or Astra Militarum.
- Fight on 30-player or 64-player maps that evolve over days or weeks.
- Use economic control, military might and strategic alliances to dominate.
- Play across Vigilus, a vital planet in the Nachmund Gauntlet.
- Join a long-term multiplayer strategy game from the creators of Supremacy 1914.

## Pricing and What Is Still Unclear

Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000 is free to download and play. The official page states that some game items can also be purchased for real money. It adds that you can set up password protection for purchases in your Google Play Store settings if you do not want to use that feature.

The page does not show the price of those items, whether a subscription exists, or whether a trial is offered. It also does not list supported devices, regions or languages. The single biggest limitation is that nothing has been released, so no one can say how the game actually plays.

If you want a finished game today, skip this and wait. If you prefer a solo campaign over diplomacy with dozens of strangers, this is not built for you. If you enjoy long multiplayer strategy rounds, pre-registering costs nothing and only gets you a notification.

Pre-register on Google Play if the faction and map scale appeal to you. Otherwise, wait for a confirmed release date before spending any attention on it.
