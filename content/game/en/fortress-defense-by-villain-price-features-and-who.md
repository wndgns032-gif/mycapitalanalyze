## What Fortress Defense by Villain Is
Fortress Defense by Villain is a 3D strategy defense game for iOS. You build an army from eight base unit types, including infantry, heavy armor, armored cars, artillery, and support units. You place durable troops at the front and protect supporting units behind them. The game asks you to balance income and survival: you can summon additional enemies to increase your income, but that raises the pressure you face. You also upgrade your fortress health, attack, and defense, and spend mana on skills that support your army. Your worker's movement, placement, and skills are part of the plan too. A hands-on tutorial teaches the real controls. Progress is saved when you clear a round, so you can resume later.
