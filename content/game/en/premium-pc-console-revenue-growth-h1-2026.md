## Premium Market Hits $13.8B as Steam Playtime Diversifies

Combined premium game revenue across PlayStation, Steam, and Xbox climbed 5% year-over-year to $13.8 billion in the first half of 2026, according to a Sensor Tower report published by GameLook. Spending held uniform across the half, recording $6.9 billion in Q1 and $6.9 billion in Q2, after an $8.8 billion peak in Q4 2025. Steam premium playtime grew 13%, driven by action titles surging 28% and strategy games rising 24%. Premium shooters grew only 14%, while RPG playtime dropped 14%, and sports and racing recovered in Q2. Pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI（GTA 6）jumped from a $2.4 million to $3.8 million daily baseline to $16.3 million the day after Netflix broadcast its preview special "An Extended Look," with PlayStation driving $12.9 million and Xbox $3.4 million. Daily pre-orders settled at $5.9 million two weeks later.

## Ad Spend Narrowing to Meta and Streaming Reshapes Marketing

These shifts force publishers to adjust genre production schedules and advertising allocations. The 28% surge in action playtime and 24% rise in strategy show PC players favoring deep, replayable titles, while the 14% contraction in RPG engagement signals player fatigue from existing backlogs. Premium shooter developers also face resistance, as the category’s 14% growth lags far behind its free-to-play footprint.

Media planners are reacting by concentrating ad budgets. In the United States, PC and console publishers placed over 76% of social media marketing spend across Meta platforms and YouTube. Instagram and Facebook captured over 50%, YouTube took slightly under 25%, and TikTok lifted the top four platforms past 91%. Reddit secured nearly 5% of social ad spend, multiples higher than its mobile footprint. The sixfold pre-order spike for Grand Theft Auto VI confirms non-gaming entertainment partnerships can stimulate direct console software sales far beyond traditional video ads.

## Key Benchmarks for Q3 Platform Revenue and Fall Campaigns

To measure whether this 5% market expansion holds, observers should track several concrete milestones through late 2026. First, monitor Q3 and Q4 platform disclosures to see if combined quarterly revenue maintains the $6.9 billion baseline, or if software releases slip into early 2027. Second, watch whether rival publishers negotiate broadcast preview agreements with video streaming services following Take-Two’s success with Netflix. Finally, track whether Reddit retains its 5% social ad capture during Q4 marketing pushes, and examine Steam autumn analytics to verify if RPG playtime stabilizes after the 14% first-half decline.
