## What Kingshot is
Kingshot is an idle medieval survival game that mixes strategic management with base defense. You play as a governor after a rebellion has collapsed the dynasty, and you must lead survivors through invasions, disease and resource shortages. The official description says you will devise internal and diplomatic strategies to rebuild civilization. The game is free to download on Google Play and is developed by Century Games PTE. LTD. It is a released title, not a pre-order, so you can start playing today.

## Key features
- Defend against invasions: Gather resources, upgrade defenses and repel attacks to protect your town.
- Manage human resources: Assign survivors as workers, hunters or chefs, and monitor their health and happiness.
- Establish laws: Create codes of law to sustain civilization and grow your town.
- Recruit heroes: Collect unique heroes with various talents and skills to strengthen your squads.
- Forge alliances: Join or form alliances to ease survival and collaborate with other players.
- Compete with other governors: Challenge rivals, earn points and rare items, and climb the rankings.
- Advance technology: Reclaim lost tech fragments to gain an edge in the race for cutting-edge technology.

## Who it is for and who should skip
Kingshot is aimed at players who enjoy idle progression, base building and competitive strategy against other players. If you like games where you check in regularly to manage resources and heroes, this fits that pattern. However, you should skip Kingshot if you prefer single-player experiences without alliances or leaderboards. It is also not for you if you dislike idle mechanics that require patience and routine attention. The game's description emphasizes competition and alliances, so solo players may find it less appealing. If you want a game with no social or competitive pressure, look elsewhere.
