## Key features
- Carry over progress: load your progress from the previous game 'PICK ME CHANCE' and start as a Ranker from day one.
- Idle growth: log in and watch your character grow, with progression that continues even in a busy life.
- Farming dungeons and rankings: explore dungeons to collect enhancement materials and items, defeat giant bosses and climb rankings.
- PvP Battle Royale: fight other players in an arena where yesterday's ally is today's enemy.
- Guild system: join Guild Alliance Wars and defeat bosses alongside guild members.
- Cyberpunk ninja setting: command shadow-moving ninjas in a city lost to the zombie virus.
