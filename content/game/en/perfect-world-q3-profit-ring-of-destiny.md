## Quarterly Performance Highlights
Perfect World announced that its Q3 2026 net profit rose 211.47% year‑on‑year to 5.06 billion RMB, a turnaround after a 41.83% decline in the first nine months. The surge is attributed to the global public test of Ring of Destiny (异环), which has logged more than 27 billion RMB in cumulative revenue as of 28 October 2026, with roughly 60% coming from official PC and Android channels and overseas revenue outpacing domestic sales.  Century Huatong led the sector with 332.98 billion RMB of revenue for the first three quarters, up 22.31% YoY, and posted a net profit of 63.35 billion RMB, a 45.37% increase. Kaixin Network (恺英网络) posted 78.11% revenue growth to 72.58 billion RMB and a net profit of 20.69 billion RMB, while Giant Interactive (巨人网络) doubled both revenue (98.45% to 66.85 billion RMB) and profit (103.73% to 28.86 billion RMB). All four firms reported stronger operating cash flow, with Perfect World’s cash flow rising 32.87% to 11.81 billion RMB.

## Strategic Implications
The earnings lift reshapes the competitive landscape for China’s top publishers. Perfect World’s reliance on a single new IP demonstrates that a successful global launch can reverse a multi‑quarter profit slump, prompting peers to accelerate overseas marketing spend. Kaixin’s announced investment of US$298 million (≈20.30 billion RMB) to acquire a 49% economic interest in 40.25% of Wemade’s equity creates a cross‑border IP pipeline for the “Legend” series, potentially altering licensing dynamics in both Chinese and Korean markets. Century Huatong’s continued cash‑flow strength gives it leeway to increase sales and R&D outlays, while Giant’s 27.66% rise in R&D spending signals a push toward next‑gen titles. Investors will likely reassess valuation models that previously discounted Chinese publishers for weak new‑product pipelines.

## Upcoming Milestones
Watch for Kaixin Network’s completion of the Wemade stake, slated for the end of Q4 2026, which will trigger a mandatory disclosure under Chinese foreign‑investment rules. Perfect World’s Q4 earnings release, expected in early February 2027, will reveal whether Ring of Destiny can sustain its revenue momentum and whether the company can return to profit growth for the full year. Additionally, analysts will monitor Century Huatong’s next‑quarter marketing budget filings, as a further rise could indicate a broader industry shift toward aggressive global promotion of flagship titles.
