Random Ability Tower Defense is an upcoming roguelike tower defense game from developer Pham Tuong Van. Instead of placing many towers, you build a single tower and roll random abilities for it, upgrading them from rank F to EX. It is listed as free on the Apple App Store and is not released yet.

The game is listed for pre-order with a release date of 17 October 2026. It sits in the Games category under Casual and Strategy, and the listed languages are English, Portuguese and Spanish. The official page does not show a rating, a download count or any player feedback yet.

## What Random Ability Tower Defense Is

Random Ability Tower Defense is a tower defense strategy game about the build rather than the map. Enemies never walk past your tower; they circle it forever, and every lap they survive makes them tougher. If the ring fills up, the run ends, so the pressure is to kill fast.

Each run gives you random abilities and random ranks, which the listing compares to a roguelite deck builder. You can sell and reroll for free before wave 1, or switch to the Modern ruleset to pick 1 of 3 abilities and merge items. The page does not show how long a full run takes beyond the Quick mode estimate.

## Key Features

- Roll 28 random abilities and Supers, from poison stacking to chain lightning and summoned tanks.
- Face bosses, mutations and Uber enemies that boost, heal and shield their army every lap.
- Pick a mode: Tutorial, Quick at 50 waves, Classic at 100 waves, or Endless.
- Switch between top-down 2D and a 3D voxel arena at any time, even mid-run.
- Race 2 to 8 friends over local Wi-Fi with identical waves and no accounts.
- Use the built-in mod editor to draw textures, edit models and share .ratdpack files.

## Pricing and What Is Still Unclear

The price is listed as free, with no energy and no timers. Optional ads can unlock a revive, a free roll or 30 minutes without ads, so the page shows ads rather than a subscription or a paid tier.

The listing does not state whether in-app purchases exist, whether a trial applies, or what anything costs beyond free. An internet connection is required to start a game, which is the biggest practical catch for anyone expecting offline play. Players who want a fixed map, no randomness or no ads should skip this one.

Pre-ordering costs nothing beyond the free price and reserves the download for 17 October 2026. Anyone who dislikes random rolls, needs offline play or wants a finished 3D mode should wait for launch details instead.
