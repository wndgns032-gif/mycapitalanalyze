TheEmberlingsEndlessGrove is a pixel survival game built around one job: keeping two fires alive at the same time. You burn nearby fuel to refill your own FIRE, collect wood and carry it back to camp, and the run ends the moment either flame dies. It is not released yet, so nobody has played it and no session length, control detail or supported device list has been confirmed beyond the store entry.

The goal of a watch is to restore the campfire to 60/60. Every trip into the grove trades your own reserves for wood, and the longer you stay out, the less you have left to come home with. The game is free to download and is listed for iPhone and iPad, with English as the only supported language.

## What TheEmberlingsEndlessGrove Is and What You Actually Do

A run is a loop of burning, gathering and returning. You stand beside a wood cache while it burns to collect wood, then carry it back and feed the camp to push the fire toward 60/60. SPLIT lets you leave a small flame guarding camp while you explore, and you choose how much FIRE to give up before you go.

Mosslings push toward you as pressure rises, and BURST is the tool the description names against them. Water is an obstacle to route around, so timing and path choice matter more as a watch goes on. The material does not state how long a single watch lasts, and it does not describe the touch controls beyond calling them touch controls.
