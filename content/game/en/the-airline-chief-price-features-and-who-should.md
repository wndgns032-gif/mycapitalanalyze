## What The Airline Chief Is

The Airline Chief is a business simulation game for people who follow commercial aviation. You start with one aircraft and a loan, then build an airline that competes against up to 14 rival carriers. The rivals grow, start price wars and sometimes go bankrupt.

The listing places it in the Games category under Simulation and Strategy, and it supports English, French and German. The game runs on iPhone and iPad, and it needs an internet connection when an ad is due, which is every eighth game week. No other platforms are named on the official page.

## Key Features

Start with one aircraft and a loan, then open routes between real airports.

Buy, lease or trade used aircraft and configure cabins.

Plan every flight of the week and manage fleet maintenance.

Set economy, business and first class fares route by route.

Use over 1,100 real airports, traffic rights, codeshares and alliances.

Manage fuel, staff, lounges, marketing, loans, taxes and a board of directors.
