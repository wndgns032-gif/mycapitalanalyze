Hug the Dot is a minimalist action game that challenges you to keep your finger on a moving dot. The game uses a single‑touch control scheme and offers daily courses that are identical for every player. It promises replay reviews so you can see exactly where you slipped.

The app is listed as free and includes occasional ads between runs. It is currently in pre‑order status with a release date set for 2026‑10‑29, according to the official Apple Store page. No account or personal data is required to play.

## What Hug the Dot Is
The official description says, “Stay on the dot. A dot wanders across the screen, a little faster every second.” You press and hold the dot, following it as it speeds up. Pickups appear around the arena and modify the dot’s size, speed or multiplier. Losing the dot ends the run and shows a replay of your finger’s path. Each day offers one fixed course that all players attempt.

## Key Features
The game highlights several core features:
- Single‑touch control that lets you press and hold the moving dot.
- Daily courses that are identical for every player worldwide.
- Pickups that double multiplier, grow, shrink, or speed up the dot.
- Replay view showing both dot and finger paths after a loss.
- Offline mode for random courses; online connection needed for daily challenges.
