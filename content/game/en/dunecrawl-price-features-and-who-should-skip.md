## what DuneCrawl is
DuneCrawl is an open world action-adventure game where you and up to three friends explore the sandy seas atop a gigantic Crab Crawler. The premise is unusual: your vehicle is a living crustacean, and the world is a desert ocean dotted with oasis islands and devious dungeons. Combat happens both on deck and on foot, and the goal is to help your fellow Polloi pilgrims win their war against the Ceramicist's invasion. The listing also confirms a solo route, where you take to the wastelands by yourself with a bit of help from a ghostly companion.

- Adventure in local or online co-op with up to 4 players, or play solo with a ghostly companion.
