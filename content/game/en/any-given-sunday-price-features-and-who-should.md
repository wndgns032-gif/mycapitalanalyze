Any Given Sunday is an upcoming pro football management game from developer Eric Nelson. It is listed on the App Store at $9.99 and is available for pre-order ahead of a release date of 2026-10-22. The game is positioned as a text-based management sim in the spirit of late-90s classics, with you as head coach and general manager of one of 32 clubs.

The official page confirms it is single player and offline, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It supports English only and runs on iPhone and iPad by touch. The listing does not show a rating, a download count or any user scores, so there is no public feedback to weigh yet.

## What Any Given Sunday Is

Any Given Sunday is a pro football management game where you run one of 32 clubs as head coach and general manager. You live every day of the league year, from training camp through the season, playoffs, free agency and the draft. Match days can be followed in text commentary, play by play, or in a 2D view showing all 22 players. You can let your coordinators call the game or call every play yourself from your own playbook.

The store page describes a deep roster system with every player rated from 1 to 20 on 32 attributes, seen through your scouts' eyes until you know him well. It also confirms a real salary cap with signing bonuses, dead money, restructures, the franchise tag, the rookie scale, fifth-year options and restricted free agent tenders. The official page does not show a rating or any user scores for Any Given Sunday.

## Key Features

Any Given Sunday packs a full management suite into a phone and tablet interface. The official page lists these concrete items.

- Manage one of 32 clubs across training camp, season, playoffs, free agency and the draft.
- Follow games in text commentary, play by play, or a 2D view of all 22 players.
- Rate every player from 1 to 20 on 32 attributes through your scouts' eyes.
- Handle a real salary cap with bonuses, dead money, franchise tags and rookie scales.
- Use over 550 offensive plays from 51 formations and over 200 defensive plays.
- Edit the database, rename clubs, change colors and share packs.
