## Steam’s new stance on AI‑generated games
Valve’s Steam business team, led by Tom Giardino and Mark Wilkinson, used the Oct 27 Indie Connect event in South Korea to reaffirm a core principle: “Make the legitimate version the best version.” In a Q&A, the team explained that Valve will not remove AI‑generated titles deemed low‑quality, labeling them “AI junk games.” Instead, they urged developers facing AI‑driven plagiarism threats to build strong community ties and continuously update their games. Giardino noted that Steam’s concurrent user peak recently hit 43.5 million worldwide, with Korean sales up 126 % over five years and in‑game purchases soaring 388 %.

## Implications for developers and publishers
The guidance directly affects indie studios that fear AI‑crafted copies. Valve will not act as a gatekeeper on AI tool usage, but it encourages creators to differentiate through unique features and ongoing updates. The new Wishlist Data API, announced by Wilkinson, lets developers pull wish‑list numbers by AppID, date range, region and language—providing granular marketing insight. The Personalized Calendar, now a permanent feature after Steam Lab testing, surfaces upcoming releases to users with similar play habits, but only while a launch date is set. Both tools aim to shift exposure from raw wish‑list counts (which Giardino says do not influence visibility) to genuine player engagement. For publishers, the emphasis on community building and data‑driven promotion means reallocating resources toward post‑launch support and localized communication rather than relying on “wish‑list boosting” services.

## What to monitor
Watch for the rollout of the Wishlist Data API documentation, scheduled for release on November 15, 2026, and the first batch of games using the Personalized Calendar, expected to appear in the Steam store by early December 2026. Additionally, track any follow‑up statements from Valve regarding AI‑generated content policy, especially if developers submit formal abuse reports after the event. These milestones will indicate how quickly Valve’s new tools translate into measurable visibility gains for indie titles.
