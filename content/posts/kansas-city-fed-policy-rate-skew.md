# The Kansas City Fed's Policy Rate Skew: A Window into Market Expectations

The Kansas City Federal Reserve has developed a unique tool to gauge the financial markets' perception of risk regarding future short-term interest rates, known as the Kansas City Fed Policy Rate Skew (KC PRS). This innovative approach utilizes options prices to provide insights into the market's sentiment and expectations, which can be crucial for investors and policymakers alike.

## Key Facts

The KC PRS is a daily index that reflects the balance of risks for future short-term U.S. interest rates. It is constructed using the same methodology as the Chicago Board Options Exchange Skew Index (Cboe Skew) on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange’s options data. The index is designed to provide a forward-looking indicator of interest rate movements, offering a unique perspective on market expectations.

The KC PRS index has been available in FRED since April 3, 1989, and its value can be tracked over the past five years. The index tends to lead cyclical patterns in interest rates, meaning it can provide early signals of potential changes in the economic landscape.

A positive value of the KC PRS index suggests that financial markets expect interest rates to be higher in the future than currently projected, while a negative value indicates the opposite. Between mid-September 2022 and late March 2026, the index generally showed values below zero, indicating a market expectation of lower interest rates. However, as of early 2026, the index has averaged values above zero, suggesting a shift in market sentiment.

## Analysis

The use of options prices to measure financial risk is a testament to the evolving nature of economic analysis. By incorporating the forward-looking nature of options trading, the KC PRS offers a nuanced view of market expectations that goes beyond traditional economic indicators.

The index's ability to lead cyclical patterns in interest rates is particularly valuable. It provides a forward-looking signal that can help investors and policymakers anticipate changes in the economic environment. For instance, if the KC PRS indicates a potential rise in interest rates, investors may adjust their portfolios accordingly, while policymakers may take this into account when making decisions about monetary policy.

## Implications

The implications of the KC PRS for markets, investors, and policy are multifaceted:

### For Markets

The KC PRS can serve as a valuable tool for market participants to gauge the potential direction of interest rates. This information can be used to inform investment strategies, particularly in sectors sensitive to interest rate changes, such as real estate and fixed-income securities.

### For Investors

Investors can use the KC PRS to gain insights into market sentiment and adjust their portfolios accordingly. For example, if the index indicates a potential rise in interest rates, investors may consider shifting their allocations away from fixed-income assets and towards equities or other interest rate-sensitive instruments.

### For Policy

Policymakers can use the KC PRS as an additional tool to assess the economic outlook and inform their decisions regarding monetary policy. The index's forward-looking nature can provide valuable information about market expectations and help policymakers anticipate potential economic challenges.

In conclusion, the Kansas City Fed's Policy Rate Skew is a valuable tool for understanding market expectations and anticipating future economic trends. Its unique approach to measuring financial risk using options prices offers a forward-looking perspective that can be crucial for investors and policymakers alike. As the economy continues to evolve, the KC PRS is likely to remain an important indicator for those seeking to navigate the complex financial landscape.
