Followers Tracker For Insta! Review: Features, Price & Release Date

Followers Tracker For Insta! is a free iOS app from Lean Apps that tracks new followers, unfollowers and audience growth for Instagram accounts.

Source: App Store

Followers Tracker For Insta! is a free iOS app from developer Lean Apps that helps you keep an eye on changes in your Instagram audience. It sits in the Entertainment category and is also tagged under Productivity, which fits a tool built around monitoring follower activity rather than posting or editing content.

The app was released on September 30, 2026, so it is still very new. That matters for anyone reading this: there is not much public user feedback to draw on yet, and the official description is the main source of detail about what the app does. What follows is based on that listing rather than on hands-on testing.

What Followers Tracker For Insta! Is

Followers Tracker For Insta! is an independent tracking tool for Instagram accounts. According to its App Store listing, it lets you monitor follower activity and get insights into how your audience changes over time. The core idea is simple: instead of checking your profile and guessing what changed, the app collects follower activity into a view you can review.

The listing names four tracking jobs in particular. You can track new followers, monitor unfollowers, view follower growth insights, and analyze your Instagram audience. Those four items cover both directions of change, people arriving and people leaving, plus a broader look at how the numbers move.

One point the developer makes clearly is that the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Instagram or Meta. It is a third-party tool, and the listing presents it as such. That is worth knowing before you connect any account, because it means support and data handling come from Lean Apps, not from Instagram.

The listing also links to a privacy policy and terms of use on the developer's site, which is where you would look for details on how account data is handled. The official App Store page is available here: App Store.

Key Features

The feature set described by the developer is focused and easy to summarize. The app tracks new followers, so you can see who has started following you. It monitors unfollowers, so you can see who has stopped. It provides follower growth insights, which put those changes into a broader picture of how your account is trending. And it offers audience analysis, aimed at helping you better understand who your followers are.

Around those four functions, the listing emphasizes a simple and easy-to-use interface. That is the only design claim made in the official description, and it lines up with the app's purpose: a tracker is most useful when the numbers are quick to read rather than buried under menus.

The description also frames the app as a way to stay informed about changes and to take control of your social media insights. That is the stated goal rather than a promise of specific results. No analytics formulas, data refresh intervals, or account-limit details appear in the source material, so those specifics remain unknown for now.

Who It Is For

This app is aimed at Instagram users who care about the movement of their audience. If you regularly wonder who unfollowed you, or you want a running record of new followers instead of noticing changes by chance, that is the use case the listing describes.

It also suits people who treat their Instagram account as something worth measuring. Creators, small business owners, and anyone building an audience may want growth insights and audience analysis in one place. Because the app is listed under both Entertainment and Productivity, it can be approached either as a casual curiosity tool or as part of a lightweight tracking routine.

It is less relevant for people who never check their follower counts, or who want a full social media management suite. The description covers tracking and insights only. It does not mention scheduling posts, editing photos, or managing multiple platforms, so anyone expecting those tools should look elsewhere.

Pricing and Availability

Followers Tracker For Insta! is free to download. The listing shows no price and no mention of subscriptions or in-app purchases, so the source material does not indicate any paid tiers. If a paid option exists, it is not described in the official listing.

The app is available on the App Store and requires an iPhone or iPad running iOS, since it is distributed through Apple's store. The current version is 1.0, released on September 30, 2026, which means it is a first release rather than a mature product with a long update history.

Because the app is so new, there is no meaningful body of user reviews or ratings to report. Anyone considering it should treat the official description as the primary source and check the store page for the latest version details and any changes to pricing.
