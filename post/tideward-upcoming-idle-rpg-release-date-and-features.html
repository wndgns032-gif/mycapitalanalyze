Tideward: Upcoming Idle RPG Release Date and Features

Tideward is an upcoming offline idle RPG for Apple devices with 28 skills, no ads, and one purchase. Release date set for February 11, 2027.

Source: App Store

Tideward is an upcoming idle RPG from Manu Games LLC, currently available for pre-order on the App Store. It is built for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, and Apple Watch, and it promises a peaceful, offline progression experience with no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The game is listed as version 1.0.0 and is scheduled for release on February 11, 2027.

The official description positions Tideward as a deep idle game with 28 skills across four disciplines, a single up-front purchase, and native support for every Apple device. It is not a web wrapper or a port; it is built in pure SwiftUI. As an upcoming title, all details are subject to change before launch.

What We Know So Far

According to the App Store listing, Tideward is a simulation and roleplaying game that combines idle mechanics with skill training, combat, crafting, and exploration. The core loop involves training skills, fighting monsters, gathering resources, and crafting gear. Progress continues even when the app is closed, with up to 24 hours of full-speed offline progression. The game is designed to be played at your own pace, without daily-login pressure or monetization timers.

The developer states that there are no servers, no analytics, and no crash reporters. Your save file lives in your private iCloud, end-to-end encrypted by Apple. The only telemetry is on-device and never leaves your phone. The game also integrates with Game Center for achievements and leaderboards, and includes accessibility features such as VoiceOver labels and Dynamic Type support.

Expected Release Date

The App Store listing shows a release date of February 11, 2027. The app is currently in pre-order status, meaning you can reserve it before launch. Because this is an upcoming release, the date could shift. Manu Games LLC has not disclosed a price yet, so the pre-order listing may not show a final cost.

Key Features

Tideward offers 28 skills divided into four disciplines. Combat includes eight skills covering melee, ranged, magic, faith-based buffs, vitality, defence, and a gated monster-task progression that unlocks late-game dungeons. Gathering covers woodcutting, fishing, mining, farming, and pilfering from traders. Artisan skills let you smelt and forge gear, cook, kindle fires, scribe runes, mix potions, and craft arrows, jewelry, and leather goods. Support skills include wayfaring obstacle courses, stargazing for passive buffs, and binding familiars from bears to dragons.

Every system feeds into the others. You can mine ore, smelt it into bars, forge a blade, and take it into a dungeon. The game is designed to offer the depth of an MMO with the patience of an idle game. Progression continues offline for up to 24 hours at full speed, with no throttling and no ads to claim rewards.

The game is native on every Apple device. On iPhone and iPad, it is touch-first and thumb-friendly. On Mac, it supports keyboard shortcuts, hover, and multi-window. On Apple TV, it uses the Focus Engine and Siri Remote. On Apple Vision Pro, it has a spatial layout with proper depth. On Apple Watch, it provides glanceable progress, complications, and training summaries. You buy once and play across all devices, with your character syncing through your private iCloud.

Privacy is a core design principle. There are no servers, no analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising IDs, and no third-party SDKs. The only telemetry is on-device and never leaves your phone. The game also includes full Game Center integration with achievements and leaderboards, VoiceOver labels throughout, Dynamic Type tested at the largest sizes, and support for Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.

The business model is a single purchase. There are no subscriptions, no premium currency, no loot boxes, no battle pass, and no timers asking you to spend money to skip them. The game you buy is the game you play.

Who It Is For

Tideward is for players who enjoy idle and incremental games but want a premium, offline experience without ads or microtransactions. It is also for Apple users who want a game that works across all their devices, from iPhone to Apple Watch to Apple Vision Pro. If you like skill training, crafting, and combat in a relaxed setting, this may appeal to you. It is not for players who prefer real-time action or competitive multiplayer, as the focus is on peaceful, solo progression.

Pricing

The price is not disclosed in the source material. The listing shows a pre-order option, but the final price has not been announced. The developer states it will be a single up-front purchase with no subscriptions or in-app purchases for premium currency. For now, check the App Store listing for updates.
