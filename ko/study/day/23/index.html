① 복습 — 7일 전 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. Employees ____ wish to apply for the annual leadership training program must submit their requests before Friday.

  • ① who
  • ② which
  • ③ whose
  • ④ whom
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

선행사가 사람 명사인 Employees이고, 빈칸 뒤에 동사 wish가 바로 이어지므로 주격 관계대명사 who가 정답입니다. which는 사물 선행사에 쓰이며, whose는 소유격, whom은 목적격 관계대명사입니다.

2. Mr. Henderson interviewed several candidates ____ previous work experience closely matched the firm's requirements.

  • ① who
  • ② whose
  • ③ which
  • ④ that
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

빈칸 뒤에 완전한 절의 구조(명사 previous work experience + 동사 matched + 목적어)가 이어져 선행사 candidates의 소유 관계를 나타내므로 소유격 관계대명사 whose가 알맞습니다. 주격이나 목적격 관계대명사인 who, which, that은 뒤에 불완전한 절이 와야 하므로 적절하지 않습니다.

3. The revised safety protocol outlines the specific procedures by ____ laboratory staff must handle hazardous materials.

  • ① that
  • ② which
  • ③ what
  • ④ whose
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

전치사 by 뒤에서 사물 선행사 procedures를 수식하는 관계절을 이끄는 목적격 관계대명사 which가 정답입니다. 관계대명사 that은 전치사 바로 뒤에 올 수 없으며, what은 선행사를 포함하므로 명사 뒤에 올 수 없습니다.

4. Kensington Electronics unveiled a smart thermostat ____ regulates indoor temperature automatically based on occupancy.

  • ① who
  • ② whom
  • ③ which
  • ④ what
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

사물 선행사인 a smart thermostat을 수식하며 동사 regulates의 주어 역할을 해야 하므로 사물 주격 관계대명사 which가 적절합니다. who와 whom은 사람 선행사에 쓰이고, what은 선행사를 수식하지 않고 자체에 포함하는 관계대명사입니다.

5. The keynote speaker, ____ our conference planning committee selected last month, has confirmed her attendance.

  • ① whom
  • ② whose
  • ③ which
  • ④ where
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

사람 선행사(The keynote speaker)를 보충 설명하며 관계절 내에서 타동사 selected의 목적어 역할을 하므로 목적격 관계대명사 whom이 정답입니다. whose는 소유격, which는 사물 선행사에 쓰이며, where는 장소를 나타내는 관계부사입니다.

6. What is the main purpose of the notice?

  • ① To announce temporary office adjustments due to remodeling
  • ② To recruit staff for an IT infrastructure project
  • ③ To introduce a permanent remote work policy
  • ④ To schedule an upcoming management training seminar
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

공지문 첫 문장에서 4층 관리 사무실이 10월 14일부터 정기 보수 공사를 시작한다고 밝히며 이에 따른 자리 재배치를 안내하고 있으므로 A가 정답입니다. IT 채용이나 영구 재택근무 정책, 교육 세미나에 관한 내용은 언급되지 않았습니다.

근거 “Please be advised that the fourth-floor administrative offices will undergo scheduled renovations starting Monday, October 14.”

7. Where will affected employees work during the construction?

  • ① At their home offices
  • ② In conference rooms on the second floor
  • ③ In a newly leased building downtown
  • ④ On the fourth floor in a partitioned section
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

공사 기간 동안 4층 직원 전원이 2층의 비어 있는 회의실로 임시 이전한다고 명시되어 있으므로 B가 정답입니다.

근거 “During this period, all fourth-floor staff members will be temporarily relocated to the vacant conference rooms on the second floor.”

8. What are employees instructed to do before Friday evening?

  • ① Submit their preference for desk locations
  • ② Test their computer network connection
  • ③ Pack personal items and lock away confidential files
  • ④ Return borrowed equipment to the IT department
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

직원들은 이번 주 금요일 오후 5시까지 개인 소지품을 챙기고 보안 문서를 파일 캐비닛에 보관하라는 안내가 나와 있으므로 C가 정답입니다.

근거 “Consequently, employees should pack up personal belongings and secure sensitive documents in their assigned filing cabinets by 5:00 P.M. this Friday.”

9. Who should employees contact if they have inquiries about their desk placement?

  • ① The building maintenance team
  • ② The human resources director
  • ③ An IT technician
  • ④ Their direct supervisor
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

좌석 배치에 관한 질문이 있을 경우 직속 상사(immediate supervisor)에게 문의하라고 명시되어 있으므로 D가 정답입니다.

근거 “If you have questions regarding desk assignments, please contact your immediate supervisor.”

② 복습 — 어제 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. The new software update ____ by the engineering team last night.

  • ① releases
  • ② has releasing
  • ③ was released
  • ④ released
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

정답은 A) was released 입니다. ‘by the engineering team’이라는 행위자를 나타내는 전치사구가 뒤에 오므로 수동태 형태인 ‘was released’가 필요합니다.

2. All employee manuals ____ in both English and Spanish by the end of the quarter.

  • ① are translating
  • ② will be translated
  • ③ have translated
  • ④ will translate
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

정답은 A) will be translated 입니다. ‘by the end of the quarter’라는 미래 시점을 나타내는 부사구와 함께 미래 수동태 형태가 맞습니다.

3. The conference rooms ____ regularly for cleaning by the facilities staff.

  • ① are being cleaned
  • ② have been cleaned
  • ③ clean
  • ④ were cleaned
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

정답은 A) are being cleaned 입니다. 현재 진행형 수동태를 사용해 ‘현재 청소 중이다’라는 의미를 전달합니다.

4. A special discount ____ to all members who renew their subscription before December 1.

  • ① is offering
  • ② offers
  • ③ offered
  • ④ will be offered
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

정답은 A) will be offered 입니다. ‘to all members’라는 수동의 의미와 미래 시제를 나타내기 위해 미래 수동태가 필요합니다.

5. The annual report ____ by the finance department and will be distributed next week.

  • ① has been prepared
  • ② prepared
  • ③ will prepare
  • ④ prepares
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

정답은 A) has been prepared 입니다. 현재완료 수동태를 사용해 ‘이미 준비되었다’는 의미를 전달합니다.

6. What discount is offered on all beverages during the first week?

  • ① 10% discount
  • ② 15% discount
  • ③ 20% discount
  • ④ 25% discount
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

정답은 C) 20% discount 입니다. 지문에 ‘the café will offer a 20% discount on all beverages during the first week’라고 명시되어 있습니다.

근거 “the café will offer a 20% discount on all beverages during the first week”

7. During which days and times is the discount available?

  • ① Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.
  • ② Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m.–11:00 a.m.
  • ③ Every day, 7:00 a.m.–10:00 a.m.
  • ④ Monday through Thursday, 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

정답은 B) Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m.–11:00 a.m. 입니다. 지문에 ‘from Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.’라고 적혀 있습니다.

근거 “from Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.”

8. What do customers receive when they join the Brew Club loyalty program?

  • ① A free coffee each month
  • ② A complimentary pastry with any coffee purchase
  • ③ A discount on all food items
  • ④ A gift card worth $10
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

정답은 B) A complimentary pastry with any coffee purchase 입니다. 지문에 ‘customers who sign up for the Brew Club loyalty program will receive a complimentary pastry with any coffee purchase’라고 명시되어 있습니다.

근거 “will receive a complimentary pastry with any coffee purchase”

9. How can customers obtain more information about the café?

  • ① Only by visiting the website
  • ② Only by calling the phone number
  • ③ By visiting the website or calling the phone number
  • ④ By sending a fax to the café
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

정답은 C) By visiting the website or calling the phone number 입니다. 지문에 ‘visit our website at www.morningbrew.com or call (555) 123‑4567’라고 적혀 있습니다.

근거 “visit our website at www.morningbrew.com or call (555) 123‑4567”

③ 오늘 새 내용 약 20분

Part 5 — to부정사와 동명사

1. The company encourages employees ____ professional development courses.

  • ① to attend
  • ② to attending
  • ③ attending
  • ④ attend
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘encourage’ 뒤에는 to부정사가 올 때가 많습니다. 따라서 ‘to attend’가 올바른 형태입니다.

2. She denied ____ the documents before the meeting.

  • ① read
  • ② to reading
  • ③ reading
  • ④ to read
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘deny’는 뒤에 동명사를 취합니다. ‘reading’이 문법적으로 맞습니다.

3. We plan ____ the new software next month.

  • ① to implementing
  • ② implementing
  • ③ to implement
  • ④ implement
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘plan’ 뒤에는 보통 to부정사가 따라옵니다. ‘to implement’가 정답입니다.

4. He admitted ____ the error in the calculation.

  • ① making
  • ② to making
  • ③ make
  • ④ to make
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘admit’는 동명사를 요구합니다. 따라서 ‘making’이 올바른 형태입니다.

5. The board decided ____ the proposal after reviewing the data.

  • ① to reject
  • ② reject
  • ③ to rejecting
  • ④ rejecting
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘decide’ 뒤에는 to부정사가 쓰입니다. ‘to reject’가 문법에 맞습니다.

Part 7 — 기사문

City Launches Bike‑Share Program to Ease Traffic

The municipal government announced today the launch of a new bike‑share program aimed at easing traffic congestion in the downtown area. The primary goal of the program is to reduce traffic congestion and promote environmentally friendly transportation. The city will deploy 2,500 bicycles across 150 stations, strategically placed near office complexes, shopping centers, and transit hubs. Senior citizens can receive a 30% discount on monthly memberships, while university students and regular commuters will pay the standard rate. The service will operate 24 hours a day, with bikes equipped with GPS tracking and electric assist for uphill routes. The program is expected to be fully operational by the end of March, with a promotional event scheduled for next week. Officials hope that the initiative will encourage more residents to choose biking over driving, thereby improving air quality and reducing commute times.

1. What is the primary goal of the new bike‑share program?

  • ① Reduce traffic congestion
  • ② Expand subway lines
  • ③ Promote car sales
  • ④ Increase tourism
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

지문에 ‘The primary goal of the program is to reduce traffic congestion…’이라고 명시되어 있어, 교통 혼잡 감소가 목표임을 알 수 있습니다.

근거 “The primary goal of the program is to reduce traffic congestion and promote environmentally friendly transportation.”

2. How many bikes will be available at the start of the program?

  • ① 2,500
  • ② 5,000
  • ③ 3,800
  • ④ 1,200
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

본문에 ‘The city will deploy 2,500 bicycles across 150 stations…’이라는 문장이 있어, 초기 배치 수는 2,500대임을 확인할 수 있습니다.

근거 “The city will deploy 2,500 bicycles across 150 stations, strategically placed near office complexes, shopping centers, and transit hubs.”

3. Which group is offered a discounted membership?

  • ① Public school teachers
  • ② University students
  • ③ All residents
  • ④ Senior citizens
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘Senior citizens can receive a 30% discount on monthly memberships’라는 문장에서 해당 할인 대상이 시니어 시민임을 알 수 있습니다.

근거 “Senior citizens can receive a 30% discount on monthly memberships,”

4. When will the program be fully operational?

  • ① By the end of the year
  • ② In June
  • ③ In September
  • ④ By the end of March
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘The program is expected to be fully operational by the end of March’라는 구절이 정확한 시점을 제시합니다.

근거 “The program is expected to be fully operational by the end of March,”
