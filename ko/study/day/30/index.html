① 복습 — 7일 전 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. The company encourages employees ____ professional development courses.

  • ① to attend
  • ② to attending
  • ③ attending
  • ④ attend
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘encourage’ 뒤에는 to부정사가 올 때가 많습니다. 따라서 ‘to attend’가 올바른 형태입니다.

2. She denied ____ the documents before the meeting.

  • ① read
  • ② to reading
  • ③ reading
  • ④ to read
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘deny’는 뒤에 동명사를 취합니다. ‘reading’이 문법적으로 맞습니다.

3. We plan ____ the new software next month.

  • ① to implementing
  • ② implementing
  • ③ to implement
  • ④ implement
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘plan’ 뒤에는 보통 to부정사가 따라옵니다. ‘to implement’가 정답입니다.

4. He admitted ____ the error in the calculation.

  • ① making
  • ② to making
  • ③ make
  • ④ to make
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘admit’는 동명사를 요구합니다. 따라서 ‘making’이 올바른 형태입니다.

5. The board decided ____ the proposal after reviewing the data.

  • ① to reject
  • ② reject
  • ③ to rejecting
  • ④ rejecting
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘decide’ 뒤에는 to부정사가 쓰입니다. ‘to reject’가 문법에 맞습니다.

6. What is the primary goal of the new bike‑share program?

  • ① Reduce traffic congestion
  • ② Expand subway lines
  • ③ Promote car sales
  • ④ Increase tourism
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

지문에 ‘The primary goal of the program is to reduce traffic congestion…’이라고 명시되어 있어, 교통 혼잡 감소가 목표임을 알 수 있습니다.

근거 “The primary goal of the program is to reduce traffic congestion and promote environmentally friendly transportation.”

7. How many bikes will be available at the start of the program?

  • ① 2,500
  • ② 5,000
  • ③ 3,800
  • ④ 1,200
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

본문에 ‘The city will deploy 2,500 bicycles across 150 stations…’이라는 문장이 있어, 초기 배치 수는 2,500대임을 확인할 수 있습니다.

근거 “The city will deploy 2,500 bicycles across 150 stations, strategically placed near office complexes, shopping centers, and transit hubs.”

8. Which group is offered a discounted membership?

  • ① Public school teachers
  • ② University students
  • ③ All residents
  • ④ Senior citizens
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘Senior citizens can receive a 30% discount on monthly memberships’라는 문장에서 해당 할인 대상이 시니어 시민임을 알 수 있습니다.

근거 “Senior citizens can receive a 30% discount on monthly memberships,”

9. When will the program be fully operational?

  • ① By the end of the year
  • ② In June
  • ③ In September
  • ④ By the end of March
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘The program is expected to be fully operational by the end of March’라는 구절이 정확한 시점을 제시합니다.

근거 “The program is expected to be fully operational by the end of March,”

② 복습 — 어제 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. Having completed the quarterly report, the manager ____ the team to prepare for the upcoming audit.

  • ① asking
  • ② will ask
  • ③ asked
  • ④ asks
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘Having completed…’은 과거 완료형 분사구문이며, 주절도 과거 시제로 일치해야 하므로 ‘asked’가 정답이다.

2. The new software, ___ by the IT department, improves data processing speed by 30%.

  • ① develop
  • ② developing
  • ③ develops
  • ④ developed
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘by the IT department’ 뒤에 과거분사가 와야 하므로 ‘developed’가 올바른 선택이다.

3. ___ the deadline approaching, many employees stayed late to finish their tasks.

  • ① Having
  • ② Because
  • ③ With
  • ④ Since
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘With the deadline approaching’은 현재분사구문으로, ‘With’를 사용해 전체 문장을 연결한다.

4. The conference, ___ in Tokyo last year, will be held in Seoul this time.

  • ① helded
  • ② holding
  • ③ held
  • ④ having held
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘held in Tokyo last year’는 과거분사 형태가 필요하므로 ‘held’가 정답이다.

5. ___ the new policy, the company expects to reduce expenses significantly.

  • ① Implements
  • ② Implemented
  • ③ Implements
  • ④ Implementing
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘Implementing the new policy’는 현재분사구문으로, 주절의 동작이 앞으로 일어날 것을 나타낸다.

6. What is the main purpose of this email?

  • ① To request a report
  • ② To announce a new product launch
  • ③ To provide the schedule for an upcoming conference
  • ④ To inform about a policy change
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

이메일은 회의 일정을 안내하고 참석을 요청하는 것이 목적이므로 B가 정답이다.

근거 “We are pleased to announce the schedule for the International Conference 2024, which will take place in Seoul from November 12 to November 15.”

7. On which day will the Panel Discussion be held?

  • ① November 13
  • ② November 15
  • ③ November 12
  • ④ November 14
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

일정표에 ‘November 14 – 09:00–12:00 Panel Discussion’이라고 명시되어 있다.

근거 “November 14 – 09:00–12:00 Panel Discussion: Future of Global Trade”

8. By what date should employees confirm their attendance?

  • ① November 10
  • ② October 20
  • ③ November 1
  • ④ October 5
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

이메일 본문에 참석 확인 마감일이 ‘October 20’이라고 적혀 있다.

근거 “Please confirm your attendance by replying to this email by October 20.”

9. Who should be contacted for questions about the event?

  • ① Lee Min-ho
  • ② Global Corp HR
  • ③ Jae Kim
  • ④ The IT department
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

문의 담당자는 ‘Lee Min-ho’이며, 이메일 주소도 함께 제공된다.

근거 “For any questions, contact the event coordinator, Lee Min-ho, at minho.lee@globalcorp.com.”

  • ③ for
  • ④ about

3. Please send the revised contract ____ email by the end of the day.

  • ④ through

4. The conference will be held ____ the new headquarters.

  • ② on

  • ④ to

5. She was promoted ____ recognition of her outstanding performance.

Part 7 — 이메일

Request for Updated Project Timeline

Subject: Request for Updated Project Timeline Dear Mr. Lee, I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to ask for an updated timeline for the Alpha‑Beta integration project. As we discussed in last week’s meeting, the client has requested a revised delivery date due to recent changes in their internal schedule. Could you please provide the new milestones, including the expected completion date for the testing phase, by Thursday, March 12? Additionally, if there are any resource constraints that might affect the schedule, kindly let us know so we can adjust our plans accordingly. Your prompt response will help us keep the client informed and maintain our commitment to quality service. Thank you for your cooperation. Best regards, Sofia Martinez Project Coordinator

1. What does the writer request from Mr. Lee?

  • ① A meeting agenda
  • ② A budget report
  • ③ An updated timeline
  • ④ A personnel list
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

본문에서 ‘I am writing to ask for an updated timeline…’이라고 명시되어 있어 최신 일정표를 요청하는 것이 맞습니다.

근거 “I am writing to ask for an updated timeline for the Alpha‑Beta integration project.”

2. By when does the writer need the new milestones?

  • ① Monday, March 9
  • ② Tuesday, March 10
  • ③ Friday, March 13
  • ④ Thursday, March 12
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘by Thursday, March 12’라는 문구가 정확한 마감일을 제시하고 있습니다.

근거 “by Thursday, March 12”

3. What specific phase's completion date is mentioned?

  • ① Design phase
  • ② Testing phase
  • ③ Implementation phase
  • ④ Review phase
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

본문에 ‘expected completion date for the testing phase’라고 명시되어 있어 테스트 단계가 언급됩니다.

근거 “expected completion date for the testing phase”

4. Why does the writer emphasize a prompt response?

  • ① To reduce costs
  • ② To keep the client informed
  • ③ To schedule a celebration
  • ④ To meet internal deadlines
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

‘Your prompt response will help us keep the client informed’이라는 문장에서 고객에게 정보를 제공하기 위해 신속한 회신이 필요함을 알 수 있습니다.

근거 “Your prompt response will help us keep the client informed”

farther vs further
