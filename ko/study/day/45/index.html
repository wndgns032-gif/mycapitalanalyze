1. The new policy ___ already clarifies the guidelines for remote work.

  • ① will
  • ② have
  • ③ had
  • ④ has
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘already’는 조동사 뒤에 위치해야 하므로 ‘has already’가 올바른 형태입니다.

2. Our project manager ___ will soon announce the next phase.

  • ① has
  • ② will
  • ③ have
  • ④ had
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

‘soon’은 조동사 ‘will’ 뒤에 와야 하므로 ‘will soon’이 맞습니다.

3. The marketing team ___ frequently updates the website with new content.

  • ① has
  • ② have
  • ③ did
  • ④ does
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘frequently’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 위치해 ‘does frequently’가 올바른 형태입니다.

4. The supplier ___ always delivers the parts on time.

  • ① has
  • ② did
  • ③ does
  • ④ have
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘always’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 배치되어 ‘does always’가 됩니다.

5. The manager ___ usually arrives early for meetings.

  • ① does
  • ② did
  • ③ has
  • ④ have
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘usually’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 위치하므로 ‘does usually’가 맞습니다.

6. What is the primary purpose of the email?

  • ① To announce a new product
  • ② To share quarterly sales figures
  • ③ To request a report from department heads
  • ④ To provide the schedule for an upcoming meeting
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

이메일은 회의 일정을 안내하기 위해 발송되었습니다.

근거 “Please review the schedule below for the product launch coordination meeting next week.”

7. At what time does the Marketing presentation begin?

  • ① 09:00
  • ② 09:30
  • ③ 10:15
  • ④ 13:00
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

일정표에 ‘Marketing presentation’은 09:30에 시작한다고 명시되어 있습니다.

근거 “09:30 – 10:15 Marketing presentation”

8. Which group is required to attend the Engineering update?

  • ① Only the Engineering team
  • ② Marketing and Sales staff only
  • ③ All department heads
  • ④ External consultants
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘Attendance is mandatory for all department heads’라고 명시되어 있어 모든 부서장이 참석해야 합니다.

근거 “Attendance is mandatory for all department heads.”

9. When is the coffee break scheduled?

  • ① 09:30 – 10:15
  • ② 14:30 – 15:00
  • ③ 12:00 – 13:00
  • ④ 10:15 – 10:30

정답 ④

근거 “10:15 – 10:30 Coffee break”
