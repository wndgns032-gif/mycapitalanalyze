① 복습 — 7일 전 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. The new policy ___ already clarifies the guidelines for remote work.

  • ① will
  • ② have
  • ③ had
  • ④ has
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘already’는 조동사 뒤에 위치해야 하므로 ‘has already’가 올바른 형태입니다.

2. Our project manager ___ will soon announce the next phase.

  • ① has
  • ② will
  • ③ have
  • ④ had
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

‘soon’은 조동사 ‘will’ 뒤에 와야 하므로 ‘will soon’이 맞습니다.

3. The marketing team ___ frequently updates the website with new content.

  • ① has
  • ② have
  • ③ did
  • ④ does
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘frequently’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 위치해 ‘does frequently’가 올바른 형태입니다.

4. The supplier ___ always delivers the parts on time.

  • ① has
  • ② did
  • ③ does
  • ④ have
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘always’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 배치되어 ‘does always’가 됩니다.

5. The manager ___ usually arrives early for meetings.

  • ① does
  • ② did
  • ③ has
  • ④ have
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘usually’는 조동사 ‘does’ 뒤에 위치하므로 ‘does usually’가 맞습니다.

6. What is the primary purpose of the email?

  • ① To announce a new product
  • ② To share quarterly sales figures
  • ③ To request a report from department heads
  • ④ To provide the schedule for an upcoming meeting
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

이메일은 회의 일정을 안내하기 위해 발송되었습니다.

근거 “Please review the schedule below for the product launch coordination meeting next week.”

7. At what time does the Marketing presentation begin?

  • ① 09:00
  • ② 09:30
  • ③ 10:15
  • ④ 13:00
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

일정표에 ‘Marketing presentation’은 09:30에 시작한다고 명시되어 있습니다.

근거 “09:30 – 10:15 Marketing presentation”

8. Which group is required to attend the Engineering update?

  • ① Only the Engineering team
  • ② Marketing and Sales staff only
  • ③ All department heads
  • ④ External consultants
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘Attendance is mandatory for all department heads’라고 명시되어 있어 모든 부서장이 참석해야 합니다.

근거 “Attendance is mandatory for all department heads.”

9. When is the coffee break scheduled?

  • ① 09:30 – 10:15
  • ② 14:30 – 15:00
  • ③ 12:00 – 13:00
  • ④ 10:15 – 10:30
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

일정표에 ‘Coffee break’은 10:15부터 10:30까지로 적혀 있습니다.

근거 “10:15 – 10:30 Coffee break”

② 복습 — 어제 약 5분

TOEIC · 9문항 — 문제를 다시 풀고 정답을 확인하세요.

1. The manager ____ the report by Friday.

  • ① will have completed
  • ② completes
  • ③ had completed
  • ④ has completed
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘by Friday’는 미래 시점을 나타내므로 미래완료형인 ‘will have completed’가 시제에 맞는다.

2. When the sales team ____ the new pricing structure, the customers will notice lower rates.

  • ① had implemented
  • ② will implement
  • ③ implemented
  • ④ implements
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘when’ 절은 현재형을 사용하고, 주절은 미래형이므로 ‘implements’가 올바른 시제이다.

3. By the time the conference ____ , we ____ all the promotional materials.

  • ① starts / will have prepared
  • ② will have started / will have prepared
  • ③ started / had prepared
  • ④ will start / have prepared
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

‘by the time’ 뒤에는 현재형이 오고, 그 앞절에 대비해 ‘will have prepared’와 같이 미래완료형을 써야 시제 일치가 맞는다.

4. If the supplier ____ the shipment earlier, we ____ avoid the delay.

  • ① ships / can avoid
  • ② shipped / could avoid
  • ③ had shipped / could have avoided
  • ④ will ship / will avoid
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

가정법 과거완료 ‘had shipped’와 결과에 대한 과거가능성 ‘could have avoided’가 시제 일치를 이루며, 다른 선택지는 시제 형태가 맞지 않는다.

5. The client says that the contract ____ next month, so we ____ the final draft now.

  • ① was signed / will prepare
  • ② has signed / prepare
  • ③ is signed / prepared
  • ④ will be signed / should prepare
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘says that’ 뒤에는 미래 사실을 나타내므로 ‘will be signed’가 맞고, 현재 시점에서 해야 할 일은 ‘should prepare’이다.

6. What is the main purpose of this email?

  • ① To cancel the meeting
  • ② To request a report
  • ③ To reschedule the meeting
  • ④ To introduce a new project
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

이메일은 회의 일정을 ‘has been moved’라고 밝히며 일정을 변경한다는 것이 주된 목적이다.

근거 “the project kick‑off meeting originally planned for Tuesday, March 12 at 10:00 a.m. has been moved to Wednesday, March 13 at 2:00 p.m.”

7. By when should the recipients confirm their availability?

  • ① By next Monday
  • ② By the end of the week
  • ③ By the end of the day tomorrow
  • ④ No confirmation is required
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘Please confirm your availability by the end of the day tomorrow’ 라는 문장에서 확인 기한을 정확히 알 수 있다.

근거 “Please confirm your availability by the end of the day tomorrow”

8. Which topics will be covered in the meeting?

  • ① Marketing strategy and sales forecast
  • ② Client feedback and product testing
  • ③ Budget, staffing, and timeline
  • ④ Project timeline, deliverables, and roles
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘The agenda will remain the same, covering the project timeline, deliverables, and roles’ 라는 문장에서 회의 안건을 확인할 수 있다.

근거 “covering the project timeline, deliverables, and roles”

9. If a recipient cannot attend the new time, what should they do?

  • ① Cancel all other meetings
  • ② Attend anyway
  • ③ Ignore the change
  • ④ Let the sender know as soon as possible
정답·해설 보기

정답 ④

‘If you have any concerns about the new time, let me know as soon as possible’ 라는 문장이 바로 그 지시이다.

근거 “let me know as soon as possible”

③ 오늘 새 내용 약 20분

Part 5 — 가정법

1. If the manager ____ the report by Friday, we could finalize the budget.

  • ① had submitted
  • ② would have submitted
  • ③ will submit
  • ④ submits
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

이 문장은 과거에 일어나지 않은 상황을 가정하고 있으므로 가정법 과거완료 형태인 'had submitted'가 정답이다.

2. Were the client to agree to the new terms, the project ____ start next month.

  • ① could
  • ② will
  • ③ would
  • ④ should
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

조건절에 'were'가 생략된 가정법 현재형이며, 결과는 가능성을 나타내는 'could'가 맞다.

3. If we ____ more time, we would have completed the training program.

  • ① were having
  • ② have had
  • ③ had
  • ④ have
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

과거에 더 많은 시간이 있었다면이라는 가정이므로 과거형 'had'를 사용한다.

4. Had the system ____ earlier, we could have avoided the outage.

  • ① to upgrade
  • ② upgraded
  • ③ been upgraded
  • ④ upgrading
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

‘Had … been upgraded’는 가정법 과거완료 구조이며, ‘been upgraded’가 올바른 형태이다.

5. If the supplier ____ a discount, we might be able to lower the product price.

  • ① will offer
  • ② offered
  • ③ had offered
  • ④ offers
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

현재 시점의 가정을 나타내므로 과거형 'offered'가 적절하다.

Part 7 — 사내 공지

Upcoming System Maintenance and Updated Password Policy

To: All Employees From: IT Department Date: November 27, 2026 Subject: Scheduled System Maintenance and Updated Password Policy Dear Team, Please be informed that a scheduled maintenance of the corporate network will take place on Saturday, December 3, from 02:00 AM to 06:00 AM (local time). During this window, all internal servers, email services, and shared drives will be temporarily unavailable. We recommend that you save your work and log out of the system before the maintenance begins. Additionally, starting December 10, the company will enforce a new password policy requiring a minimum of twelve characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Failure to comply with the new policy may result in temporary account restrictions. For assistance, contact the IT Help Desk at extension 1234. Thank you for your cooperation.

1. When will the corporate network be unavailable due to maintenance?

  • ① Saturday, December 3, 02:00-06:00 AM
  • ② Sunday, December 4, 02:00-06:00 AM
  • ③ Saturday, December 10, 02:00-06:00 AM
  • ④ Friday, December 2, 02:00-06:00 AM
정답·해설 보기

정답 ①

공지문에 네트워크 점검이 12월 3일 토요일 오전 2시부터 6시까지 진행된다고 명시되어 있다.

근거 “scheduled maintenance of the corporate network will take place on Saturday, December 3, from 02:00 AM to 06:00 AM”

2. What is the minimum number of characters required for the new password?

  • ① 8 characters
  • ② 10 characters
  • ③ 12 characters
  • ④ 14 characters
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

새 비밀번호 정책에서 최소 12자 이상이어야 한다고 규정하고 있다.

근거 “minimum of twelve characters”

3. How often must employees change their passwords under the new policy?

  • ① Every 30 days
  • ② Every 60 days
  • ③ Every 90 days
  • ④ Every 120 days
정답·해설 보기

정답 ③

공지문에 비밀번호는 90일마다 변경해야 한다고 명시되어 있다.

근거 “Passwords must be changed every 90 days.”

4. What consequence is mentioned for not complying with the new password policy?

  • ① Permanent account deletion
  • ② Temporary account restrictions
  • ③ Salary reduction
  • ④ No consequence
정답·해설 보기

정답 ②

정책을 따르지 않을 경우 일시적인 계정 제한이 발생할 수 있다고 안내하고 있다.

근거 “may result in temporary account restrictions”

④ 오늘의 토익 단어 매일 3개

  • turnover명

    매출액 · 이직률

    Annual turnover rose 12 percent.

  • concise형

    간결한

    Keep the report concise.

  • accommodate동

    수용하다 · 숙박을 제공하다

    The hotel can accommodate 500 guests.

헷갈리는 표현

adapt vs adopt

adapt = 적응하다·개조하다 / adopt = 채택하다·입양하다

암기 팁 · 접속사 vs 전치사: 빈칸 뒤에 '주어+동사'가 오면 접속사(Since), 명사만 오면 전치사(Because of)입니다.
