About

MyCapital Analyze is an independent publication focused on macroeconomics, financial markets, and capital flows. We cover the forces that move the global economy — interest rates, inflation, growth, and the structural shifts reshaping how capital is allocated.

Our content is produced through a combination of data analysis and AI-assisted curation of public information. Each article is reviewed for accuracy and clarity, and is written to be accessible to both professionals and curious readers.

Our Approach

  • Independent: We are not affiliated with any financial institution, and we do not accept payment to promote specific securities or products.
  • Evidence-based: Analysis is grounded in public data, official statistics, and verifiable sources.
  • Clear: We avoid jargon where plain language will do, without sacrificing analytical rigor.

Important Disclaimer

All content on this site is provided for informational and educational purposes only. It does not constitute investment, financial, or legal advice. Always conduct your own research and consult a licensed professional before making investment decisions.
