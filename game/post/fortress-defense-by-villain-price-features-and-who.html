    • What Fortress Defense by Villain Is

    Fortress Defense by Villain is a 3D strategy defense game for iOS. You build an army from eight base unit types, including infantry, heavy armor, armored cars, artillery, and support units. You place durable troops at the front and protect supporting units behind them. The game asks you to balance income and survival: you can summon additional enemies to increase your income, but that raises the pressure you face. You also upgrade your fortress health, attack, and defense, and spend mana on skills that support your army. Your worker's movement, placement, and skills are part of the plan too. A hands-on tutorial teaches the real controls. Progress is saved when you clear a round, so you can resume later.

    Key Features

    • Combine eight base unit types, including infantry, heavy armor, armored cars, artillery, and support units.
    • Choose from two promotion paths for each unit type, with units growing to stage three.

      • Who It Is For and Who Should Skip

      Fortress Defense by Villain is for players who enjoy formation-based strategy, unit upgrades, and wave defense. It suits people who like planning around income and survival trade-offs, and who are comfortable with a free-to-play structure that includes optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases. You should skip it if you want a game you can buy once and own completely, or if you dislike energy systems. Starting a new game uses energy, and energy replenishes at a set time each day. That means you cannot play unlimited sessions back-to-back without waiting or watching optional ads. If that model does not appeal to you, this is not the game for you.
