    • What Sum Circuit is

    Sum Circuit is a puzzle game in the Games and Puzzle genres, published by Sae Won Yang and available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and iPod touch. The core loop is arithmetic and gravity: you select 2–4 neighbouring tiles whose values add up to the target sum, connect them, and those tiles clear. Because numbers above fall into the gaps, clearing a tile now can change what is available for the next target. You work through targets from left to right to light the circuit.

    The listing describes three ways to play. The 30-stage Journey opens with a short interactive tutorial and then moves through multiple targets and different connection shapes. The 365-puzzle Expedition unlocks one circuit at a time, asks you to complete each sector, and saves your best star result for every puzzle. Daily and Bonus circuits give you a date-based puzzle, and completing the Daily Circuit unlocks its bonus for free.

    Controls are deliberately plain: tap tiles and press Connect, or drag across them and release. Undo and restart are available when a route dead-ends. Modes, stage selection and game tools live in Settings rather than on the play screen, so the game screen stays focused. You can also repeat the tutorial without losing your current puzzle.

      Who it is for and who should skip

      Sum Circuit suits players who want a calm, offline number puzzle they can pick up in short sessions. Progress, completed-stage records and puzzle state are stored on your device, and no account is required, so it works on a commute or a flight. The 365-puzzle Expedition gives that kind of player a long, steady ladder to climb, and the saved best star result per puzzle gives a reason to return to a stage.

      Skip Sum Circuit if you want a competitive puzzle game. The listing shows no leaderboards, no timers, no multiplayer and no online component beyond ad delivery. Skip it too if you need a large body of player opinion before spending time on a puzzle game: Sum Circuit carries a 0.0 out of 5 rating from 0 ratings, so there is no user consensus to check. Players who dislike ads in any form should also weigh the hint system carefully, even though the ads are optional.

      What to expect

There is no mention of a story, characters, level themes or progression systems beyond stages, sectors and star results, so the appeal rests on the sum-matching mechanic itself and on the order in which you clear tiles. Expect the difficulty to come from planning ahead: the listing warns that a tile you clear now might be needed for the next target, which points to puzzles that punish careless clearing rather than slow tapping.

      Expect a small, quiet interface. Offline play with no account means progress lives on your device, so there is no cloud profile described in the listing.
