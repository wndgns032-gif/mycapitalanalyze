    • What WFL - World Football Legends Is

    WFL - World Football Legends is a football game from developer Rana Muhammad Anas, filed under the Games category with the Simulation and Sports genres attached to it. The listing describes a loop that starts with squad building: you create a custom team by selecting players from the available roster, and that squad then travels with you into the rest of the game. The official description frames it as putting you in control of your own football experience, with the closing line stating that it is your football team and your match.

    The structure is deliberately narrow. There is no career mode, no transfer market and no club ownership layer described in the listing. The description repeats the build-then-compete idea in several places, which suggests the custom squad is the core hook rather than a side feature.

    WFL - World Football Legends is also positioned as a returning-habit game rather than a one-session game. Daily challenges, achievements and daily rewards are all listed as separate features, and the description explicitly says to keep coming back for new objectives and to return each day to collect rewards. It also does not describe online multiplayer, cross-play or a ranked ladder. Anyone expecting a licensed football product should read the description carefully before committing.

    Key Features

    • Team building: select players from the available roster and assemble your own custom football squad, which then carries across the game's modes.
    • Four match modes: Kick Off for a straight match, League for competition against other teams, Tournament for knockout-style progression, and Penalty Kicks for finishing practice from the spot.
