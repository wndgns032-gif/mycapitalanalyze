What Whisperborne Is

Whisperborne is a classic pixel JRPG built around a story-driven journey across the continent of Mira. The official description says Elle claims the sealed holy blade Marintica and begins her journey with a whisper: “I can hear the wind.” Her bond with Sophia, a girl lost 3,000 years ago, is described as deciding the fate of the world.

The game is presented as a classic pixel JRPG with detailed high-res pixel art and lyrical staging, in the spirit of Octopath Traveler. The listing names turn-based combat, ally and monster collection, co-op raids, dungeons and mini-games such as fishing. It is a single upcoming title from Neptune Company, and the page does not show a demo or a beta.
